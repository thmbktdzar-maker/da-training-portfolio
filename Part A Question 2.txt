## Measurement notes

- **Rating is ordinal.** A 5-star rating ranks above a 4-star rating, but the gaps between adjacent ratings are not guaranteed to represent equal differences in satisfaction. The median (and mode) are suitable summaries; the median preserves the rating order without assuming equal spacing. Do not interpret a mean as a precise average distance unless you explicitly accept the equal-spacing assumption.
- **Dates and timestamps are interval.** Differences such as elapsed days or minutes are meaningful, while the calendar origin is arbitrary. Money and counts are ratio measures because zero represents none of the measured quantity.
- Customer, order, product, review, and line-item IDs are classified as nominal: their numeric appearance does not make them quantities.
